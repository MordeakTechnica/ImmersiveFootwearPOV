Real-Time First-Person, Third-Person (ITP), & Photo Mode Height Elevation for High Heels & Platform Boots
100% Native REDscript & TweakXL  •  Supports Vanilla Slots, Wardrobe/Transmog, & Equipment-EX

In vanilla Cyberpunk 2077, equipping 12 cm stilettos or chunky platform boots doesn't change V's height—causing soles to sink into the pavement and your first-person eye-line to remain at barefoot height.

Immersive Footwear POV reads TweakDB tags (or community/user calibrations) for your equipped footwear and dynamically elevates V's skeleton, camera stance, third-person puppet, and Photo Mode pose in real time.

  • Immersive First Person Height: Automatically raises V's eye level and stance in first-person based on the heel height of your shoes (supports Immersive First Person).
  • Third-Person Platform Lift: Lifts V cleanly in third-person (Immersive Third Person and Negotiable Third Person) so thick platform soles rest naturally on the ground instead of sinking into the pavement.
  • Photo Mode Support: Automatically adjusts V's height in Photo Mode so platform boots and chunky soles sit flush on the floor.
  • Heel-Based Movement Adjustment: Adds subtle, natural hip sway and movement while walking in heels, scaling gently with low, mid, and high heels.
  • Integrated Live Shoe Calibrator UI: Includes a built-in in-game menu (Num 1 default key) to fine-tune and save custom heel and platform heights while viewing your character up close.
  • Smart Automatic Reset: Smoothly returns V to normal height when getting into vehicles, sitting on chairs or beds, talking in story scenes, climbing ladders, or swimming.
  • Immersive Footwear POV (Main Mod): Core REDscript mod, RCF settings, Live Shoe Calibrator, First-Person & Photo Mode elevation, and community presets. (Choose this if you only play FPP / Photo Mode).
  • Immersive Footwear POV - ITP Elevation: AnimGraph archive (##_ITP_Immersive_Footwear_POV.archive) required for real-time Third-Person (Immersive Third Person) elevation.
  • Immersive Footwear POV - AllInOne: Contains both the Main Mod and the ITP Elevation archive in a single package.

Required Dependencies

  • RED4ext
  • redscript
  • ArchiveXL
  • TweakXL
  • RedFunctions, Redscript Configuration Framework (RCF), & RedLogger (DigitalVixen Mod Suite)

Recommended / Optional Integrations

  • Codeware — Powers the native Live Shoe Calibrator popup & manual cm inputs
  • RedWindows — Powers the Diagnostics HUD and fallback Calibrator window
  • Equipment-EX — Full support for Equipment-EX feet/outfit layers
  • Immersive First Person / Immersive Third Person / Negotiable Third Person
  1. Equip your shoes (via Inventory, Vanilla Wardrobe, or Equipment-EX).
  2. Press Num 1 (or open via Pause Menu → Mods → Immersive Footwear POV) to open the Live Shoe Calibrator.
  3. In Third-Person (or with Boot Focus), adjust Platform Sole (cm) until the front sole sits flush on the ground.
  4. In First-Person, adjust Heel Height (cm) to match your desired eye-line elevation.
  5. Click SAVE OVERRIDE to persist your calibration permanently to user_shoe_overrides.json.

Adding support to your custom shoe mod takes less than a minute—just append 1 or 2 tags to your item's TweakXL .yaml:

Items.Your_Custom_Shoe_Item:
  $base: Items.GenericFootClothing
  tags: !append-once
    - "HeelHeight_012"       # Total heel elevation: 12 cm (FPP eye-line & stance)
    - "PlatformHeight_004"   # Front sole thickness: 4 cm (TPP & Photo Mode puppet lift)

Supported Number Formats (up to 40 cm Heel / 30 cm Platform; FPP IFP capped at 25 cm):

  • Centimeters (Recommended): HeelHeight_008 (8 cm), HeelHeight_012 (12 cm), PlatformHeight_004 (4 cm)
  • Millimeters: HeelHeight_80 (80 mm), PlatformHeight_40 (40 mm)
  • Decimal Meters: HeelHeight_0.08 (0.08 m), PlatformHeight_0.04 (0.04 m)

Supported Tag Aliases:

  • Heel: HeelHeight_XXX, Heel_XXX, ShoeHeel_XXX, ShoeHeight_XXX, FootwearHeight_XXX
  • Platform: PlatformHeight_XXX, Platform_XXX, ShoePlatform_XXX
