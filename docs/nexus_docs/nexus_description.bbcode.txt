[img]https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/MordeakTechnica/ImmersiveFootwearPOV@master/docs/nexus_docs/images/header_overview.png[/img]
In vanilla Cyberpunk 2077, equipping 12 cm stilettos or chunky platform boots doesn't change V's height—causing soles to sink into the pavement and your first-person eye-line to remain at barefoot height.

[b]Immersive Footwear POV[/b] reads TweakDB tags (or community/user calibrations) for your equipped footwear and dynamically elevates V's skeleton, camera stance, third-person puppet, and Photo Mode pose in real time.

[img]https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/MordeakTechnica/ImmersiveFootwearPOV@master/docs/nexus_docs/images/header_key_features.png[/img]
[list]
[*][b]Immersive First Person Height:[/b] Automatically raises V's eye level and stance in first-person based on the heel height of your shoes (supports [i]Immersive First Person[/i]).
[*][b]Third-Person Platform Lift:[/b] Lifts V cleanly in third-person ([i]Immersive Third Person[/i] and [i]Negotiable Third Person[/i]) so thick platform soles rest naturally on the ground instead of sinking into the pavement.
[*][b]Photo Mode Support:[/b] Automatically adjusts V's height in Photo Mode so platform boots and chunky soles sit flush on the floor.
[*][b]Heel-Based Movement Adjustment:[/b] Adds subtle, natural hip sway and movement while walking in heels, scaling gently with low, mid, and high heels.
[*][b]Integrated Live Shoe Calibrator UI:[/b] Includes a built-in in-game menu ([b]Num 1[/b] default key) to fine-tune and save custom heel and platform heights while viewing your character up close.
[*][b]Smart Automatic Reset:[/b] Smoothly returns V to normal height when getting into vehicles, sitting on chairs or beds, talking in story scenes, climbing ladders, or swimming.
[/list]

[list=1]
[*]Equip your shoes (via Inventory, Vanilla Wardrobe, or Equipment-EX).
[*]Press [b]Num 1[/b] (or open via Pause Menu → Mods → Immersive Footwear POV) to open the [b]Live Shoe Calibrator[/b].
[*]In Third-Person (or with Boot Focus), adjust [b]Platform Sole (cm)[/b] until the front sole sits flush on the ground.
[*]In First-Person, adjust [b]Heel Height (cm)[/b] to match your desired eye-line elevation.
[*]Click [b]SAVE OVERRIDE[/b] to persist your calibration permanently to [code]user_shoe_overrides.json[/code].
[/list]

Adding support to your custom shoe mod takes less than a minute—just append 1 or 2 tags to your item's TweakXL [code].yaml[/code]:

[code]Items.Your_Custom_Shoe_Item:
  $base: Items.GenericFootClothing
  tags: !append-once
    - "HeelHeight_012"       # Total heel elevation: 12 cm (FPP eye-line & stance)
    - "PlatformHeight_004"   # Front sole thickness: 4 cm (TPP & Photo Mode puppet lift)[/code]

[spoiler=Click for Supported Tag Formats & Examples]
